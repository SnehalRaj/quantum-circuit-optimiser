# Frontier runner status

The runner supports pinned circuit recipes, source edits, stage receipts, and public frontier checks.
It can prepare a candidate, record development checks, reserve fresh audit inputs, and export a qualified result.
The [campaign guide](FRONTIER-CAMPAIGN.md) describes the commands and restart rules.

The [examples](../examples/frontier/README.md) contain replay jobs and configuration templates.
Historical jobs retain their prior outcomes and block automatic execution until those outcomes are reconciled.
Reconstruction of a known recipe measures reproduction. A discovery claim requires a new proposal record and independent checks.

The [test ledger](TEST_LEDGER.md) links completed checks and the evidence that defines their scope.
The [manuscript](../paper/LLM_Assisted_Quantum_Circuit_Optimisation.pdf) reports the frozen frontier snapshot.
